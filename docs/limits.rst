What this comparison cannot tell you
====================================

* **Quality per dollar.** Price sheets say nothing about whether a model can do the
  task. Benchmark a sample of real inputs before switching.
* **Latency.** Time to first token and tokens per second vary by provider, region
  and load, and they are usually what users notice.
* **Rate limits and quotas.** A cheap model you cannot call at peak is not cheap.
* **Contract pricing.** Enterprise agreements are private and usually below list.

Use the calculator to bound the decision, then measure the shortlist on your own
traffic.
